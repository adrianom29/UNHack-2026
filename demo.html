Model assumptions & interpretation

One item, one day, one preparation decision. D=max(0,X), where X~N(μ,σ²). The inputs μ and σ describe demand before negative values are clipped to zero; they are not the exact moments of demand after clipping. Demand is approximated as continuous, while preparation quantities are nonnegative integers. When σ=0, demand is deterministic. Unsold portions have zero salvage value and count as waste. There are no disposal costs, stockout penalties, capacity constraints, or carryover.

Profit=p×min(Q,D)−cQ. Stockout probability=P(D>Q), not the proportion of demand left unmet. Model profit deducts the entered preparation cost but excludes fixed expenses. Maximizing expected profit may increase waste or stockout risk; all three outcomes need not improve together. Default inputs are illustrative. Applying your saved records uses observed sales as an approximate demand estimate. Neither mode demonstrates verified savings.
